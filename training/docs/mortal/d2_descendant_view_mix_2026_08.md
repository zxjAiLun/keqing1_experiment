## B250 Evaluation Result

The three fixed-seed evaluations completed with 1,000 hanchans per seed. The
primary paired comparison is computed from the same complete hanchan logs.

| Comparison | Seed means | Mean | Pooled 95% CI | Equal-seed hierarchical 95% CI |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `D2-M0` | `-1.710, -11.655, -10.530` | `-7.965` | `[-12.885, -2.939]` | `[-14.625, -0.660]` |
| `D2-70k` | `-0.765, -1.890, -2.790` | `-1.815` | `[-6.780, +3.105]` | `[-6.705, +3.270]` |
| `M0-70k` | `+0.945, +9.765, +7.740` | `+6.150` | `[+1.020, +10.965]` | `[-0.450, +12.480]` |

All three D2-M0 seed means are negative and the hierarchical interval is below
zero. D2 therefore does not repair the D1 data-route failure. D2 is closed and
must not be promoted. D2-70k also does not establish a K1-strength
continuation. M0-70k is positive in these three lineups, but its hierarchical
interval still includes zero, so this evaluation does not promote an M0
checkpoint either.

The main behavior change is a much lower fuuro rate for D2 (about 20.8% pooled)
than for M0 (about 27.0%), alongside lower agari and no compensating strength
gain. This is a descriptive readout, not a causal claim.

## Evaluation Contract

The completed evaluation used the fixed B250 random-seat lineup:

```text
70k / ext_mortal / M0_seed / D2_seed
```

The report uses `D2-M0`, `D2-70k`, and `M0-70k` separately with
complete-hanchan bootstrap and equal-seed hierarchical bootstrap. No seed was
selected from training metrics. The next experiment requires a new
pre-registered data-generation hypothesis rather than another D2 checkpoint or
another view-mix ratio.
