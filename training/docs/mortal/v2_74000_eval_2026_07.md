# V2@74000 Native Screening

## Run

- Four-player native random-seat arena
- Models: `70k`, `v4`, `V2_74000`, `v4_reference`
- Games: 250 hanchans
- Seeds: `980000` through `980249`
- Rank points: `[90, 45, 0, -135]`
- Device: CUDA / `NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060 Laptop GPU`

## Results

| Model | Avg rank | Avg pt | 1st | 2nd | 3rd | 4th | Agari | Houjuu | Fuuro | Riichi |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `70k` | 2.708 | -14.58 | 18.8% | 22.4% | 28.0% | 30.8% | 21.29% | 14.26% | 30.68% | 18.61% |
| `v4` | 2.344 | 11.88 | 27.6% | 30.0% | 22.8% | 19.6% | 23.07% | 11.18% | 28.12% | 19.95% |
| `V2_74000` | 2.488 | 0.36 | 25.2% | 26.0% | 23.6% | 25.2% | 19.47% | 12.15% | 21.21% | 18.98% |
| `v4_reference` | 2.460 | 2.34 | 28.4% | 21.6% | 25.6% | 24.4% | 22.66% | 12.85% | 29.42% | 19.58% |

V2 improves deal-in rate substantially over 70k and retains a near-v4 riichi rate, but its win rate and open-hand rate are materially lower. Its average winning value is higher (`6903`) while its total win frequency is lower. This is a distinct conservative/closed-hand style, not yet a v4 replacement.

The two v4 seats are the same checkpoint used as an ecology control. Their seat-level results vary, so this 250-hanchan run is a screening result rather than a final strength decision.

## Decision

- Keep `V2_74000` as the active trained candidate and retain `v4` as the external reference.
- Do not promote V2 over v4 from this sample.
- Do not add reviewer/teacher-CE corrections.
- Run a non-overlapping 500-hanchan native confirmation before changing the training recipe.
